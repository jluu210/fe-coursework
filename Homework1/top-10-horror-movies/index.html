My Top 10 Horror Movies

One thing in life I love are movies. They bring joy, fear, and everything in between. Some core memories of first watching a movie with a loved one still stay with us to this day. Horror movies have always been a favorite of mine. The feeling of unexpected fear from a great horror movie is exhilarating and can shake even the most veteran movie watcher to the bone. It makes me laugh how I felt after a really good jump scare or fell down a rabbit hole trying to find out who the real killer could be. Even a bad scary movie can be good if it provides a great laugh or a moment of yelling, "Don't go in there!" So, with that, here are my top 10 scary movies.

Rank Movie Release Year Synopsis Why I Like It
1 The Shining 1980 A family stays in an isolated hotel where the father slowly loses his mind. One of my first Horror Movies ever watched. I still cannot go to the bathroom where the tub has a curtain slightly open.
2 The Thing 1982 Researchers in Antarctica face a shape-shifting alien that can imitate anyone. A brillant use of isolation to stir fear and paranoia
3 The Texas Chain Saw Massacre 1974 A group of friends encounter a violent family in rural Texas. The corney dialog. Gory shock and awe.
4 Jaws 1975 A giant shark terrorizes a beach town and the people trying to stop it. You don't even notice the shark doesn't apear until the end of the movie.
5 Smile 2022 A therapist is haunted by a disturbing chain of events after a traumatic incident. Best Jumpscares of all time. Smile pulls most of it's aspects from 'it follows' but just better.
6 Alien 1979 A spaceship crew is hunted by a deadly creature lurking on board. Another detailed orientated movie which give us an incredible space escapate
7 Get Out 2017 A young man visits his girlfriend’s family and discovers something deeply unsettling.
8 Nightmare on Elmstreet 1984 Teenager Nancy Thompson must uncover the dark truth concealed by her parents after she and her friends become targets of the spirit of a serial killer.
9 Invasion of the Body Snatchers 1978 People are replaced by emotionless duplicates in an unsettling alien invasion.
10 The Fly 1986 A scientist’s experiment goes horribly wrong after he begins transforming into something else.
